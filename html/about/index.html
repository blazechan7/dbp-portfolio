About

This page covers who I am, my focus and approach to design, along with my resume and CV.

Who I am

I care about products where design isn’t just about how something looks, but how it actually works and feels for the user.

My interest in design started early with creating visuals like posters and banners, and over time grew into a more critical way of thinking about how things can be improved. Alongside my studies, I’ve also worked on building my own creative projects, including my personal branding as a streamer, where I explored identity, consistency, and how people connect with a visual style.

I like working iteratively, testing ideas, refining them, and pushing designs further than the first solution. For me, good design has a clear purpose, a strong identity, and makes sense in the context it’s used in.
